HOW TO DRIVE

Key turn it to START and hold it until the engine runs · Pedals touch a pedal to press it all the way, slide your finger up to ease off; let go and it springs back · Gears drag the knob through the H with the clutch held down · Lanes grab the steering wheel and turn it · Lights pull the stalk left of the wheel to flash the car ahead out of your lane. Slower cars use your lane: overtake them, a crash costs most of your speed.
Full screen on an iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen, then start it from there.
Keyboard Enter engine on/off · Space gas · Shift clutch · B brake · ↑ ↓ gears · ← → change lanes · F flash · N nitro · G fire · M next tape · 1–3 engine · Esc pause
Mouse drag the ignition key to START and hold it · drag the pedals down (they stay put, double-click lets go) · drag the gear knob through the H with the clutch down · grab the steering wheel and turn it to change lanes. Slower cars use your lane: overtake them, a crash costs most of your speed.
